## What we did

- Wrote `plan.md` and `diagram.html`, and opened the diagram in the browser.

## What we decided  (details go in decisions.md)
- The trigger is public contract awards (option C: direct winners first, subcontractor discovery in Phase 2).
- Awards from the last 60 days only, no open tenders in v1, and never pad below 20.
- General-inbox fallback for companies under 50 people when no contact is found.
- Slack review → button → Gmail draft. The system never sends.
- Success = 2–4 replies/week, tracked manually in a tracking sheet for now.

## What we learned
- Award notices name the contract holder (usually the prime), so the core Tier 1/2 subcontractor ICP often isn't visible in public data.
- It's unclear whether award notices state CPCSC applicability outright. This may need to be inferred.
- CPCSC Level 1 started appearing in select defence contracts in summer 2026 and is required at contract award.
- Proposing defaults works better for Ashwin than open-ended questions.
